Good to know.

Does SayFill work on every website?

Not yet. The MVP supports many standard visible HTML inputs and selects. CAPTCHA, protected browser pages, cross-origin frames, and complex custom widgets may not work.

Does SayFill submit my form automatically?

No. You review the proposed answers, select which fields to fill, and submit the form yourself.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the initial extension. A backend handles audio transcription and form understanding.

Is Chrome for Android supported?

This initial release is intended for desktop Firefox and Chrome. Android support has not been tested.
