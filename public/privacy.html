Privacy at SayFill

Last updated: October 24, 2026. This policy describes the initial SayFill MVP. It must be re-reviewed before any public store release.

What SayFill processes

When you start recording, SayFill sends that recording to the configured SayFill backend for transcription using Groq. When you ask SayFill to match answers, your speech transcript and the labels, types and options of supported form fields are sent to the backend and Groq to identify potential matches. We exclude values already entered in the form, and exclude common password, OTP, payment and government-ID fields.

What is stored

The initial SayFill application has no user accounts or profile database. The extension stores your chosen backend URL locally in Chrome storage. The backend does not intentionally persist audio recordings, transcripts or form responses. Infrastructure and third-party providers may process or retain operational data according to their policies.

Permissions

SayFill asks for temporary access to the active tab when you click the extension, as well as extension-side-panel, script injection and local storage permissions. It needs access to the chosen backend endpoint. It doesn't submit forms automatically.

Your controls

You choose when to record, can edit transcripts and matched answers, and select what to fill. Do not speak passwords, OTPs, payment card information or other secrets. Close the panel to stop using the tool.

AI processor

The MVP uses Groq APIs for transcription and form understanding. Review Groq's privacy policy for its handling of information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the product owner through the project's GitHub repository until a dedicated support address is set up.
